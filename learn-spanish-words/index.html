Learn Spanish words with a memory game

Lingua Pairs turns Spanish vocabulary into a memory game. Flip two cards: one shows a Spanish word, the other its meaning in the language you speak. Find the pair and the two cards merge into one picture card with a real photo, both words and the spoken Spanish word.

The Spanish list has over 2,200 words in six levels, from A1 for beginners to C1, and in eighteen categories such as food, animals, the household, verbs and society. Nouns come with their article, so you learn el or la together with the word. You can mix levels and categories yourself, mark words you already know so they stay out of your games, or import your own word list. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, and the spoken word uses the voice installed on your phone. The app menus follow your phone's language and exist in English, German, Spanish, French and Hungarian.

Free to play. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Questions and answers

Which levels and how many Spanish words are there?

Six levels (A1, A2, A2+, B1, B2 and C1) with over 2,200 words in every language, in eighteen categories. You can also build your own mix of levels and categories.

Can I hear how a Spanish word is pronounced?

Yes. When you find a pair, the app says the word out loud, and you can switch that off in Settings. It uses the Spanish text-to-speech voice installed on your phone. If no Spanish voice is installed, the app tells you and offers to install one.

Is Lingua Pairs free?

It is free to play with a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, never in the middle of a game. One purchase removes the ads for good. There is no subscription.
