Lingua PairsThe memory game that teaches you languages

Instead of two identical cards, you match a word with its translation. Learn German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Hungarian or English with photos and spoken words, in short rounds that fit a coffee break.

Free to play. Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

How it works

Pick the language you speak and the one you want to learn. Flip two cards: one is in your language, one in the new one. Find the pair and the two cards merge into one picture card with a photo, both words and the pronunciation. Seeing, reading and hearing a word together is what makes it stick.

What you get

Six languages, any pair

Learn German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Hungarian or English in any combination, such as English to German or Hungarian to Spanish. The app itself speaks English, German, Spanish, French and Hungarian and follows your phone's language.

Match a word with its translation

Flip two cards: one in your language, one in the new one. When you find the pair, the cards merge into one picture card with a real photo, both words and the spoken word, using the voices on your phone.

Three ways to play the cards

Match word with word, photo with the new word, or your own word with a photo. Know a word already? Tap I know this and it stays out of your games for as long as you choose.

Your words, your levels

More than 2,200 words per language, six levels from A1 to C1 and eighteen categories. Build your own mix, see nouns with their articles, and import your own Anki decks or any two-column word list.

Opposites and synonyms

Match two words of the language you are learning that mean the opposite or nearly the same. Each card shows the word and its meaning in your own language underneath.

For every screen, alone or with friends

Bigger screens fit bigger boards, up to 28 pairs, in bright and dark mode on phones, tablets and foldables. Earn up to three stars per board, keep a daily streak, or play online with 2 to 4 friends at one table.

Questions and answers

Which languages can I learn?

German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Hungarian and English, in any pair. You pick the language you speak and the one you are learning, for example Hungarian to Spanish or French to English.

Is Lingua Pairs free?

Yes, it is free to play with a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, never in the middle of a game. A single one-time purchase removes ads for good. There is no subscription.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account and no sign-up. Your progress stays on your device. Word lists and photos are stored in the app, so solo games work offline. Ads and online tables with friends need a connection.

Can I use my own word lists?

Yes. You can import Anki decks or any two-column word list and play them with the same cards.

Where do the words and photos come from?

Word lists and translations were created with the help of AI and checked automatically. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, with credits in the app under Settings. If you spot a mistake, write to us and we will fix it.
