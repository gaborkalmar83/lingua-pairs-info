Lingua Pairs

The memory game that teaches you Dutch.

Flip two cards. One is English, one is Dutch. Find the pair and the cards merge into one picture card with a photo, both words and the Dutch pronunciation.

Six levels from A1 to C1, twelve categories, more than 300 words. Made for phones, tablets and foldables, in bright and dark mode.
