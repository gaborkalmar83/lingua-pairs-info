Lingua Pairs

The memory game that teaches you languages.

Pick the language you speak and the one you want to learn. Flip two cards: one is in your language, one in the new one. Find the pair and the cards merge into one picture card with a photo, both words and the spoken pronunciation.

Learn German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Hungarian or English in any combination, with more than 2,200 words per language, six levels from A1 to C1 and eighteen categories. The app is available in English, German, Spanish, French and Hungarian. Made for phones, tablets and foldables, in bright and dark mode, with online play for 2 to 4 friends.

Word lists and translations were created with the help of AI and checked automatically. Spotted a mistake? Write to us.
