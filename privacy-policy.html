Lingua Pairs

By Kalmarium Services

Privacy policy. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Lingua Pairs is an Android memory card game for learning vocabulary in several languages, made by Kalmarium Services B.V., the Netherlands ("we").

What the app stores

Your settings, stars, daily streak and the words you have matched and any flashcard decks you import (for example from Anki) are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-up, and none of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device information and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings, Privacy choices. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The one-time ad-free purchase removes all ads, and with them all ad-related data collection in the app.

Playing with friends

When you play online, the app connects to our table server (Microsoft Azure, West Europe) and sends the display name you choose, the language pair of the table (for example German and English), the words of the board, the emoji reactions you send, and a random player token. The app creates that token once on your phone and keeps it; it only lets you return to your seat after a dropped connection. The server uses this data to run the match and shows it to the other players at your table. It holds all of it in memory only and writes none of it to a disk or database. A table is deleted from memory shortly after everyone has left it, and after about 45 minutes without activity at the latest. No account is needed. Your internet address is also held in memory only, to limit abuse, and the server logs contain no names or internet addresses.

Diagnostics and problem reports

From version 2.1.1, if the app crashes, the crash text (an error trace with a time stamp, the most recent crash only) is saved in a private file on your phone and the app shows a small screen offering to send it. Settings, Send diagnostics to developer does the same at any time. The report contains the app name and version, your Android version, phone make and model, your language, the banner ad status and the last crash, plus a line where you may add what you were doing. The app never sends this report itself and uploads nothing. Only if you choose to send does your own e-mail app open with a draft addressed to kalmariumservices@gmail.com, which you can read, change or discard before sending. If no e-mail app is found, the report is copied to the clipboard instead. There is no crash-reporting service and no third-party diagnostics SDK in the app.

Purchases

The ad-free purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We receive no payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether your Google account owns the purchase.

Google Play Games

The app uses Google Play Games services for achievements and leaderboards. If you are signed in to Play Games on your device, the app sends your achievements, your total number of stars and your longest daily streak to Google. Google shows them to you and, on the leaderboards, to other players together with your Play Games name and profile picture. Your Play Games profile and its data are handled by Google, see policies.google.com/privacy. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent and the game works the same. We receive none of this data ourselves.

Speech

Words are spoken by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. The app sends no audio and no text to us.

Photos

All word photos are bundled inside the app. They come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences; the credits are listed in the app under Settings, Photo credits.

Website visits

This website counts page views with a cookie-free script that stores nothing on your device and sends only the page address, the referring website and the country (derived from your network address, which is not kept). It is switched off if your browser sends Do Not Track.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
